# Virtual Learning Platform

Business Analysis & Requirements Engineering Case Study

## Executive Summary

Functional analysis project focused on the definition and design of an online learning platform for higher education.

The initiative covers the complete requirements engineering lifecycle, including business vision, scope definition, story mapping, backlog prioritization, roadmap planning, user stories, acceptance criteria, API definitions and risk analysis.

The project was refined through multiple iterations, incorporating stakeholder feedback and improvements in business alignment, traceability and communication of requirements.

---

## Business Objectives

- Improve the online learning experience.
- Provide a reliable assignment submission process.
- Increase transparency in evaluations and feedback.
- Support scalable course delivery.
